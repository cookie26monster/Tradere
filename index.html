Welcome to Sovereign Sequence

The Path Back to Self

If you've found your way here, there's probably a reason...

Maybe you're searching for something more. Maybe you're feeling a little lost, stuck overwhelmed or disconnected from yourself. Perhaps life looks fine from the outside, but inside you know something needs to change, even if you can't quite put your finger on what that is yet.

Maybe you’re not even sure what you’re looking for, you just know that the way things are going right now aren’t quite it. Or perhaps you just want to feel more like yourself again.

I get it, I’ve been there too. My own life has taken me on a continuing journey of finding the path back to myself (something I’ll share a little more about as you get to know me).

You might be questioning what you really want, wondering where you fit in amongst everything and everyone else, or simply feeling that quiet pull towards a different way of living.

You don't need to have it all figured out before you begin. That's something we can explore together.

Sometimes life gets so busy that somewhere along the way, we lose touch with ourselves.

We spend so much time doing, coping, caring, working and meeting the needs of everyone around us that our own wellbeing can quietly slip further down the list. That's where I come in.

I'm Tracey, a wellbeing coach with a background in health and a passion for looking at the whole person rather than just one part of your life in isolation….

Because no part of us exists in isolation. Our physical wellbeing can affect how we feel emotionally, our thoughts can influence how we experience the world, our beliefs can shape the choices we make, and our sense of connection and purpose can influence how fulfilled we feel. Body, mind, emotions, beliefs and spirit are all connected, when something shifts in one area, it can ripple into the others.

That’s why I believe true wellbeing is about understanding and supporting the whole of you.

My approach brings together coaching, meditation, breathwork and practical wellbeing support to help you slow things down, understand where you are, reconnect with what's important to you and create changes that genuinely fit your life.

So what does Sovereign Sequence mean?

The name Sovereign Sequence reflects the heart of what I believe and how I work.

To me, sovereignty is about personal power, having a strong relationship with yourself, knowing what matters to you, trusting your own voice and feeling able to make choices that are right for your life. It's about living more consciously within your own body, developing inner trust and living in alignment with your true authentic self.

But life has a way of pulling us away from that.

Responsibilities, relationships, expectations, old beliefs and just trying to keep everything going can gradually disconnect us from ourselves. We can find ourselves living on autopilot, following patterns we’ve outgrown or looking outside ourselves for answers we once trusted ourselves to find.

That’s where the Sequence comes in.

There isn’t one single leap that takes us back to ourselves. It’s a sequence of small steps, shifts, choices and moments of awareness. Understanding where you are. Recognising what’s no longer serving you. Reconnecting with what matters. Learning to listen to yourself again. Making changes that bring your outer life closer to who you are within.

And that sequence won’t look the same for everyone.

Your path back to self is your path back to sovereignty — and Sovereign Sequence is about supporting you through the steps that take you there.

You are at the heart of the work

I prefer to work in a client-led way, because if everyone’s path is different, it doesn’t make sense to put everyone through the same process.

I meet you where you are, and we work from there.

Sometimes you may need structure, practical tools, clear goals and accountability. At other times, you may need space to slow down, explore what’s coming up and allow the session to flow more intuitively.

We can move between the two.

Rather than trying to fit you into a particular method, you remain at the heart of the process, and I draw from the tools and approaches that best support what you need at that point in your journey.
