Offerings

Are you ready for something to change?

You don't need to arrive here knowing exactly what you need.

Maybe you're feeling stuck, overwhelmed or disconnected from yourself. Perhaps you're constantly overthinking, questioning your decisions or putting everyone else's needs before your own.

Maybe you've become so used to getting through the day that you're functioning perfectly well on the outside, but inside you know something doesn't feel quite right.

Or perhaps nothing is particularly wrong, you just have a feeling that there’s more.

More to understand about yourself. More clarity about what you want. More connection. More confidence in your own choices. More intention in the way you're living your life.

You might not know what needs to change yet. You just know that something does and that's enough of a place to start.

What could change?

I'm not here to give you all the answers or tell you how your life should look. What I can offer is a space to slow things down, look at what's really going on and begin understanding yourself on a deeper level.

Depending on what you need, our work together can help you recognise patterns and beliefs that may be keeping you stuck, understand your thoughts and emotions, reconnect with what matters to you and become clearer about the choices you want to make.

Over time, you will begin to notice the changes showing up in everyday life.

You might catch the old pattern before automatically repeating it. Pause before reacting. Say no where you once would have said yes. Make a decision without needing everyone else's reassurance. Stop seeking external validation. Notice the way you're speaking to yourself and choose something kinder. Recognise what your body is asking for. Or finally take a step towards something you've been putting off.

These might sound like small things but small shifts can change the way we experience our whole lives.

The aim isn't to create a version of you who never struggles, doubts themselves or gets knocked off course. It's to help you develop greater awareness, self-trust and a toolkit you can draw upon when life inevitably twists and turns so that you're better able to find your own way forward.

Not to become someone new, but to strengthen the relationship you have with the person who's already there.

Ways We Can Work Together

There's no single way to find your path back to yourself, which is why I offer different ways of working together.

You may want the clarity and structure of coaching, feel drawn towards working with the breath and body, or benefit from combining approaches.

If you're unsure what would suit you, we can begin exactly where you are and work it out together.

1:1 Wellbeing Coaching

A space that's completely yours.

Our one-to-one coaching sessions give you space away from the noise of everyday life to focus entirely on you.

You might bring a particular challenge, decision or goal, or you may simply know that something doesn't feel right and want the space to explore it.

My approach is client-led, so our sessions won't follow a rigid formula. Sometimes we'll use structured coaching tools, questions, goals and practical actions. At other times, the most useful thing may be to slow down, explore what's beneath the surface and follow where the conversation naturally leads.

Your Investment :     60-minute session — £70      90-minute deeper session — £90

1:1 Breathwork

Sometimes we need to move beyond thinking and reconnect with the body.

Guided conscious connected breathwork offers an opportunity to slow down, turn your attention inward and explore what you're experiencing through the breath and body.

Sessions are guided and supported, with space afterwards to gently integrate your experience.

Your Investment :     90-minute session   —   £90

Coaching + Breathwork

Bringing conversation, awareness and the body together.

This extended session gives us time to begin with coaching exploring what is happening for you, the patterns or beliefs that may be showing up and what you'd like to shift, before moving into guided breathwork.

It's an opportunity to approach what you're experiencing from more than one direction rather than relying on conversation alone.

Your Investment :     2-hour combined session   —   £120

Group Coaching + Breathwork

Connection, reflection and shared experience.

Group sessions combine coaching and guided breathwork around a particular theme, giving you the opportunity to explore your own experience while sharing space with others.

Themes might include self-trust, boundaries, beliefs, reconnecting with yourself, confidence or other areas of personal wellbeing.

Your Investment :     2-hour group session   —   £30 per person

Group Breathwork

A guided group conscious connected breathwork experience offering time to step away from everyday demands, turn your attention inward and reconnect with yourself.

Each session will be centred around a particular intention or theme.

A meditation created specifically for you.

Rather than choosing a generic recording, we can create a meditation around what you're personally working through or hoping to cultivate. Whether that's relaxation, confidence, self-trust, preparing for an experience, visualising a goal or simply reconnecting with yourself.

Meditation Library

Once purchased, they're yours to revisit whenever you need them.

Wellbeing should be accessible

I also recognise that investing in yourself financially isn't always possible, particularly during the times when support might be needed most.

That's why I keep a small number of supported spaces available for people who would genuinely benefit from working together but can't currently afford the full investment.

I also offer a monthly pay-what-you-can session, where finances don't have to be the deciding factor in whether you reach out. If you cant afford anything, don’t pay anything, there will be absolutely no judgement.
