Training, Experience & Professional Development

My approach is shaped by a combination of professional healthcare experience, formal training and my own continued exploration of whole-person wellbeing.

I've spent 30 years within caring professions, including 25 years as a Registered Nurse, giving me extensive experience of supporting people through different stages of life, health, change and vulnerability.

Alongside this, I've continued to develop my knowledge and skills across coaching, counselling, meditation, breathwork, sound, bodywork and energy-based practice, building a varied toolkit that allows me to look at wellbeing from many different perspectives.

A continually evolving toolkit

I'm naturally curious, and learning has become an ongoing part of my own path.

I continue to explore the areas that underpin my work, including human behaviour, consciousness, spirituality, the nervous system, our thoughts, beliefs and patterns, and the relationship between our physical, mental, emotional and inner worlds.

I don't believe one modality has all the answers, nor do I believe every approach will be right for every person.

Instead, I see my training and experience as a continually evolving toolkit. Different ways of supporting the whole person while remaining flexible enough to meet each individual where they are.

Some of these practices naturally lend themselves to my current online work, while others will become part of the in-person workshops, groups and experiences I offer.

Whatever approach we use, one thing remains the same:

The modality isn't at the centre of the work. You are!

My Commitment to You

I believe that supporting others also comes with a responsibility to continue doing my own inner work.

My commitment to you is that I will continue to reflect, learn and challenge myself. Looking at my own beliefs, patterns, assumptions and blind spots, so that I can remain present, self-aware and as emotionally available as possible in the space we share.

I will continue developing my knowledge and skills, while also recognising the boundaries of my own experience and the limits of what I can offer.

My role isn’t to lead you towards the answers that make sense to me, its to create space for you to discover the answers that make sense to you. I won't always get it right, but I can promise to approach our work with curiosity, integrity and a willingness to question myself as much as I encourage you to question what you believe. If I am asking you to be more conscious of yourself, I believe I have a responsibility to keep doing the same.

Ultimately, my intention is to support you in recognising your own strengths, trusting yourself more deeply and exploring what you are capable of, without my beliefs, expectations or personal story becoming a blueprint for yours.
